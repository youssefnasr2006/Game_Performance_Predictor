Introduction

The Problem Space

⚽

Why Football is Hard

Unlike basketball, football is a rare-scoring, high-variance sport. A single goal abruptly changes match trajectory, making algorithmic prediction exceptionally challenging.

🎯

Our Task

Multi-class classification across 5 major European leagues: predict match outcome as WIN  DRAW  LOSS

🚨

The Draw Blindness Problem

Draws are less frequent than decisive outcomes and share statistical similarities with both narrow wins and narrow losses. Algorithms inherently fail to isolate the "draw" profile.

Our Contributions

① Temporal Pipeline
A rigorous feature engineering pipeline using exclusively in-game performance metrics, explicitly preventing data leakage via shift(1) rolling windows.
② Draw Blindness Fix
Applied SMOTE to the training set to force spatial representation of the draw class — strictly isolated from the test set.
③ Systematic Comparison
Five distinct algorithms — Random Forest, Logistic Regression, SVM, XGBoost, Linear Regression — evaluated with GridSearchCV and Time Series CV.
④ Interactive Demo
A Flask-based live prediction engine with sliders for venue, form streak, rest days, possession, and opponent defense quality.

Discussion

Key Findings

① Resolving Draw Blindness

Baseline RF Draw Recall

0.21

Model overwhelmingly favored wins and losses, nearly ignoring draws

After SMOTE — Draw Recall

0.85

SMOTE injected interpolative synthetic draw points across continuous feature dimensions

Draw recall improved by +305% from SMOTE alone.

② The Accuracy Paradox

Applying SMOTE to Logistic Regression dropped raw accuracy from ~52% to ~50%. Yet the model transitioned from a mathematically flawed heuristic (blindly guessing majority classes) into an honest predictor.
52% → 50%
Accuracy (dropped)
↑ Macro F1
Honest multi-class performance
↑ Precision-Recall Balance
Across all three classes

③ Systemic WIN Bias

Despite intensive over-sampling, nearly all algorithms exhibited residual bias toward the Home Win. Football inherently features psychological and physiological home advantage — the algorithms accurately mirror this ground truth.

Observed in the Live Demo

Even with a form streak of −5 and 2 days rest, the model predicted WIN when opponent defense was weak (4.0 goals/game) — confirming opp_def_r5 dominance.

is_home Boundary

When offensive rolling features lie in chaotic margins, the model defaults to predicting is_home=1 as the safe prior.

Key Insight: Opponent Defense Dominance

While offensive metrics (xG, conversion rate) are historically favored by pundits, our modelling confirms that a defensively weak opponent is mathematically more actionable and predictable than a strong overarching team offense.

opp_def_r5 Explained

The 5-match rolling average of goals allowed by the opposing team. Setting this to 4.0 (terrible defense) pushed WIN probability to 44%+ even with terrible team form — confirming this feature's outsized influence on the model.

Rolling Points Split (Home/Away)

roll5_pts_home and roll5_pts_away are the #1 and #2 most important features — confirming venue-split form is more informative than aggregate form.

Streak Effect

The consecutive win/loss streak (#3 feature) captures momentum effects that rolling averages smooth over — a team on a 5-game win streak is qualitatively different from one averaging 2pts/game.
